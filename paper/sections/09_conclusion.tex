\section{Conclusion}

We formulated autonomous merchant search as exact finite-horizon stopping with
hard time, token, API-call, API-spend, and purchase-price constraints.  Remaining
resources are part of the Bellman state, and the purchase cap restricts the
admissible action set.  On the registered synthetic benchmark, binding resource
regimes change the action for the same offer.

The reusable distinction is between pricing a resource and enforcing its
feasibility.  Scalar query costs can be useful comparators, but an autonomous
agent facing actual caps must plan over what remains possible.
