No analytic or theorem-level claim is established in the current release.  The
pathwise permit-safety statement is registered as a conjecture, and focused
ledger tests are not a substitute for a proof.  A future proof must state the
host-enforcement, atomic-reservation, one-time-reconciliation, and conservative
censoring assumptions explicitly.

The Bellman recurrence in Section~3 is evaluated by exact rational computation;
its finite benchmark output is implementation validation rather than a
proof-backed research finding.
