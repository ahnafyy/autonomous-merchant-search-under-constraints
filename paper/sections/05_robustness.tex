\section{Robustness And Extensions}

The current evidence validates the recurrence and package conformance.  It does
not establish novelty or empirical robustness.  The first research check replays
common held-out query-usage traces under adaptive, equal-split, worst-case-reserve,
greedy feasible, and fixed-depth permit policies.  Experiments must vary total
budgets, perturb usage forecasts, charge planner latency, and report purchase
loss, purchase failure, timeout and truncation rates, and hard-budget violations.

Two controls are especially important.  First, every runtime must enforce each
permit before dispatch and reconcile actual usage afterward.  Second, the same
merchant outcomes and usage traces must be paired across policies.  If adaptive
allocation cannot outperform the strongest fixed feasible baseline without an
overrun, the contribution hypothesis is rejected.  These checks are pending and
are not included in the primary claim.
