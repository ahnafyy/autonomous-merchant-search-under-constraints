\section{Checks and Boundaries}
\label{sec:robustness}

The replay harness has several checks that prevent easy but misleading comparisons.
Decks have one currency; seller and company identifiers must be unique within a deck;
seller-order permutations are deterministic from the registered seed; and adaptive
policy fitting is separated from evaluation by product. Search-all and adaptive
stopping receive the same replay order and full deck horizon.

The free-recall edge case is also tested directly: with zero inspection cost, opening
every feasible card is weakly optimal. This is a model check, not an empirical claim
about a merchant's checkout system. The inspection-cost scenarios in
Table~\ref{tab:pandora-study} and the market-rate multiplier check in
Table~\ref{tab:ucp-cost-sensitivity} express a deployment tradeoff. The multiplier
check changes the complete declared cost, not token, API, or time fields separately;
it therefore distinguishes a cost-level conclusion from an arbitrary workload change.

For the UCP series, the quality analysis checks comparable-domain transitions and
flags common-mode loss. The excluded discontinuity is retained in provenance rather
than silently removed. Repeated daily decks are clustered by SKU before the split and
bootstrap, protecting against date-level pseudo-replication. This protects the
interpretation of the panel, but does not turn catalog observations into a
price-persistence or checkout experiment.

The full August--September UCP scans form a separate month-apart robustness stratum.
They must retain their own collection identity, matching rule, and quality checks;
they should not be silently mixed with the daily September panel. That analysis is
reported only after its evaluator and claim are registered.

The strongest remaining falsification is prospective: fix the identity rule,
collection window, cost model, and advantage criterion before collecting additional
seller decks. A result that changes under that exercise should stand beside this
study as a separate registered result, not be silently pooled with it.
