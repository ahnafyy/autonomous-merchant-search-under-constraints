% MERCHANT-PERMIT-OPEN-001
No substantive result is claimed in the current release.  The finite action maps
generated by the implementation are validation fixtures: showing that a policy
reacts to binding constraints is expected behavior.

The registered open question is whether adaptive pre-call allocation of
enforceable resource permits improves purchase loss or purchase success over
fixed-split and myopic feasible baselines while retaining zero hard-budget
violations.  A future positive result must use paired held-out query traces,
include planner overhead, timeouts, truncation, and forecast error, and compare
against equal-split, worst-case-reserve, greedy feasible, and clairvoyant controls.
Any total-budget violation is a correctness failure rather than an objective
tradeoff.

Until those standards are met and the closest safe-inference and online-allocation
literature is resolved, claim \texttt{MERCHANT-PERMIT-OPEN-001} remains \emph{open}.
