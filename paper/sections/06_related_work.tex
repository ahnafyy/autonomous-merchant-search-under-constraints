\section{Related Work}

The closest mechanisms are optimal stopping with sampling costs, consumer price
search, Pandora-style costly inspection, secretary and prophet models with
irrevocable selection, and online allocation under resource capacities.  The
present model combines adaptively selected ephemeral offers with a terminal
no-purchase penalty and a vector of hard query resources.  Unlike classical
reservation-price search with recall, a rejected offer cannot be selected
later.  Unlike scalar sampling-cost models, feasibility depends on the full
remaining-budget state.

This section records mechanism overlap rather than a novelty claim.  Candidate
works and verified metadata are maintained in
\texttt{research/literature.yml}; full-text comparison and the human novelty
gate remain pending.  No theorem or unique model combination is claimed on the
basis of the current review.
