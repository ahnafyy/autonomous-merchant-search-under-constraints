\subsection{Shopify Global Catalog seller decks}

The primary policy benchmark begins with a fixed Shopify Global Catalog snapshot.
We retain USD offers that pass the collection eligibility filters and build product
decks only where the configured title-normalization rule yields at least three seller
cards. A card records a seller domain and price; a deck is the unit resampled for
evaluation. Seller-order permutations are repeated counterfactual replays, not
additional products.

The deck rule uses exact normalized model titles after an earlier UPID-plus-option
rule yielded no decks. That revision occurred after the collection and is retained in
the study record. A Shopify shop domain is a seller identifier for replay, not
proof of an independent company, inventory source, or checkout offer. Likewise,
title matching does not establish full variant equivalence, tax, shipping, returns,
or current availability.

% Claim SHOPIFY-SELLER-DECK-STUDY-001.
The registered descriptive claim is only that the frozen collection produces the
generated set of replayable decks under these rules. It makes no population or policy
superiority claim.

\subsection{UCP direct-merchant panels}

The direct-merchant series repeatedly observes read-only merchant catalog surfaces
discovered through UCP capability records. For each date, the replay constructs a
deck when a SKU has USD offers from at least three merchant domains. The same SKU
can therefore yield repeated daily decks, while all of its dates remain together in
either calibration or held-out evaluation. This supplies a temporal costly-search
study distinct from, and not pooled with, the Shopify snapshot.

% Claim UCP-OBSERVATION-QUALITY-001.
The lifecycle-quality analysis explicitly excludes dates with common-mode retrieval
discontinuities. This matters because a collector failure affecting many domains is
missing observation, not synchronized merchant delisting. UCP cards represent
catalog observations rather than checkout offers; same-SKU matching does not establish
shipping, tax, returns, or availability equivalence.
