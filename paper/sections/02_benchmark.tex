\section{Minimal Benchmark}

An episode contains a known set of \ResultMerchantCount{} unqueried merchants.
The planner chooses among feasible merchants adaptively; a fixed external order
is retained as a control.
Querying merchant $t$ consumes an integer resource vector
$c_t=(c_t^{\mathrm{time}},c_t^{\mathrm{token}},c_t^{\mathrm{call}},
c_t^{\mathrm{spend}})$ and reveals either unavailability or one landed price.
Available offers are ephemeral: an offer that is not accepted immediately
cannot be recalled.  Exhausting the merchant sequence or losing query
feasibility without purchasing incurs the registered failure penalty.
The current validation fixture records discrete merchant-specific price distributions,
stockout mass, and query requirements.  It evaluates
\ResultHardConstraintScenarioCount{} declared operating regimes on the same
forecasts and the same observed offer of \$\ResultObservedPrice{}.  The regimes
include relaxed resources, individually binding time, token, API-call, and
API-spend limits, a combined limit, and a purchase-price cap of
\$\ResultPriceCappedLimit{}.  The complete inputs are generated in
\texttt{artifacts/results.json}; they are synthetic benchmark values rather
than market estimates.

The planned empirical benchmark instead groups exact SKUs across eligible,
permission-checked merchants exposed through Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP)
endpoints.  UCP is the proposed data-access specification, not part of the
optimization problem.  Early randomized rounds calibrate forecasts;
chronologically later rounds are frozen as held-out panels before policy
comparison.  Each policy receives the same latent panel while outcomes from
unqueried merchants remain hidden until its episode ends.  A declared-capability
candidate corpus derived from live UCP protocol probing is available
(Appendix~B), but a permission-verified, calibrated panel with a registered
primary outcome is not yet included.

The validation outcome is the exact action---buy, continue, or reject without a
feasible next query---together with the reservation price and remaining budget.
The future primary empirical outcome and tolerance are not yet registered.
Planned reports include purchase success, oracle hit, item-price regret,
failure-penalized loss, savings captured, and resource-efficiency curves.  Landed
price will be used only when shipping and tax are observed consistently.
