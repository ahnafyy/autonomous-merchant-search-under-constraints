the JSON, CSV, \LaTeX{}, site, claim, and conformance artifacts.  Run

the hard-constraint claim has no floating tolerance.  Generated manifests omit
wall-clock timestamps and record the configured seed

admissibility, budget-dependent Bellman actions, deterministic artifact
generation, and claim evaluation.  Exhaustive independent policy enumeration

a completed verification result.
