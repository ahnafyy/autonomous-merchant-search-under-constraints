\section{Limitations And Open Questions}

The benchmark is synthetic and its forecast weights are not calibrated to a
merchant market.  Forecasts are known, discrete, and independent across the
fixed query order.  Query requirements are deterministic, offers cannot be
recalled, and the objective uses a declared terminal failure penalty.  The
model omits merchant selection, parallel calls, belief updating, substitutions,
shipping quality, trust, returns, strategic pricing, and execution failures.

The registered scenarios also align several resource caps with a shortened
reachable horizon.  A policy that uses only the number of reachable merchants
may therefore explain the current action differences.  Establishing that
resource identity matters requires crossed budgets with equal reachable depth,
paired policy comparisons, and forecast perturbations.  Real trace calibration
is required before interpreting any action frequency or savings estimate as an
empirical statement about autonomous shopping.
